How this is worked out. No model, only arithmetic you can check. Expected hires: {{ org.open_requests | num }} staffing requests are open. Each counts for its chance of being filled by {{ org.quarter_ends_on | d }}: of the {{ history.n | num }} requests filled in the last year, the share that were still open at this request's age and then filled by the age it will be at quarter end. (Half of all requests fill within {{ history.median | round | int }} days; the middle half take {{ history.p25 | round | int }} to {{ history.p75 | round | int }}.) Expected exits: {{ attrition.exits_60d | num }} people left {{ attrition.name }} in the last 60 days out of {{ attrition.headcount | num }}, which is {{ "%.2f" | format(attrition.monthly_rate * 100) }}% a month. That rate is applied to the people in seats for the {{ org.days_left }} days left. The range is the 25th to 75th percentile: hires are treated as independent coin flips with their own odds, and exits as a count of rare events. It does not know about reorgs, freezes or offers already signed, and it treats notice already given the same as any other exit.
