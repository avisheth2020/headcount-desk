About Headcount Desk

Headcount Desk answers one question for a fictional AI research lab called Meridian Labs: how many people can we actually hire? It turns a noisy list of positions into usable open headcount for each SVP, and every number opens the rows behind it.

It also replaces a manual backfill process (form, spreadsheet, email approval, hand-assigned recruiter) with one tracked flow that has a full audit trail. And it checks its own data every day, so you can tell a real change from a bad load.

All data is synthetic: {{ counts.people | num }} made-up people in {{ counts.positions | num }} positions across {{ counts.teams | num }} teams, with errors planted on purpose so there is something to find. Roles are simulated with the switcher in the top right; there are no real logins.

Built by Avi Sheth with FastAPI, Postgres and HTMX. The business logic is plain SQL views.
